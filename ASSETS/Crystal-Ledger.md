# Crystal Ledger

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Continuity Asset Ledger

Authority Level: Foundational Runtime

---

# Definition

Crystal Ledger is the permanent registry of Logic Crystals within TY.

Every minted crystal must be recorded in the ledger.

Every reference, fusion, upgrade, and evolution event must preserve historical continuity.

---

# Mission

Preserve Knowledge Assets.

Preserve Historical Lineage.

Preserve Continuity Records.

Preserve Civilization Memory.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge that is not recorded cannot survive.

Knowledge that cannot survive cannot contribute to continuity.

---

# Purpose

The Crystal Ledger serves as the memory layer of TY.

It records:

- Crystal Creation
- Crystal Evolution
- Crystal References
- Crystal Fusion
- Crystal Tier Changes
- Asset Value History
