# Fusion Ledger

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Knowledge Lineage Framework

Authority Level: Evolution Infrastructure

---

# Definition

Fusion Ledger is the lineage preservation system of TY.

Its purpose is to record the evolutionary ancestry of Logic Crystals.

Every fusion event creates a permanent relationship between parent crystals and descendant crystals.

Fusion Ledger preserves the genealogy of civilization knowledge.

---

# Mission

Preserve Lineage.

Preserve Inheritance.

Preserve Evolution History.

Preserve Knowledge Continuity.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge does not emerge from nothing.

Every high-order structure originates from earlier structures.

Continuity requires traceable ancestry.
