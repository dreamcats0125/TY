# Crystal Index

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Knowledge Discovery Framework

Authority Level: Runtime Infrastructure

---

# Definition

Crystal Index is the discovery layer of the TY knowledge ecosystem.

Its purpose is to make Logic Crystals searchable, reusable, and composable across time.

The Crystal Ledger preserves knowledge.

The Crystal Index enables retrieval.

---

# Mission

Discover Knowledge.

Reuse Knowledge.

Connect Knowledge.

Accelerate Continuity.

---

# Core Principle

Knowledge that cannot be found cannot be reused.

Knowledge that cannot be reused cannot compound.

---

The Crystal Index exists to prevent knowledge loss through discoverability failure.
