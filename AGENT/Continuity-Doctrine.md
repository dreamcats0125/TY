# Executive Summary

Continuity Doctrine is the philosophical foundation of the TY Agent.

Most systems optimize for growth.

Most organizations optimize for output.

Most markets optimize for efficiency.

TY optimizes for continuity.

The purpose of Continuity Doctrine is to ensure that improvement never destroys the human, the identity, or the civilization it was meant to improve.

Continuity failure occurs when:

- Identity becomes fragmented
- Memory becomes disconnected
- Meaning collapses
- Burnout exceeds recovery
- Execution exceeds sustainability
