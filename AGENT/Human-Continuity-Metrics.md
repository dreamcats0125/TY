# Executive Summary

Human Continuity Metrics (HCM) defines how TY Runtime measures human continuity.

The goal is not to measure productivity.

The goal is not to measure output.

The goal is to detect whether a human remains capable of sustaining meaningful continuity over time.

---

# Purpose

HCM exists to evaluate:

- Identity Continuity
- Memory Continuity
- Cognitive Stability
- Energy Sustainability
- Reflection Quality
