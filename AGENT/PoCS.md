# Executive Summary

Proof of Cognitive Stability (PoCS) is a framework for recognizing and preserving meaningful cognitive continuity.

PoCS does not measure productivity.

PoCS does not measure popularity.

PoCS measures whether a human mind has successfully transformed confusion into understanding while maintaining identity continuity.

---

# Purpose

PoCS exists to measure:

- Cognitive Stability
- Understanding
- Continuity
- Coherence

rather than:

- Output Volume

- Short-Term Performance
