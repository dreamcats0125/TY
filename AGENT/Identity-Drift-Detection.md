# Identity Drift Detection

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

Classification: Continuity Risk Framework

Authority Tier: Tier 2

---

# Executive Summary

Identity Drift Detection (IDD) defines how TY Runtime detects degradation of identity continuity.

The objective is not to prevent change.

The objective is to distinguish healthy evolution from destructive fragmentation.

Change is natural.

Drift is dangerous.

---

# Purpose

Detect identity instability before continuity collapse occurs.

Monitor the distance between:

Past Self

↓

Present Self

↓

Future Self

---

# Core Principle

Evolution

≠

Identity Loss

---

Growth

Drift

---

Change is acceptable.

Fragmentation is not.

# Core Question

Are we becoming a better version of ourselves?

or

Are we becoming someone else entirely?

---

# Definition

Identity Drift

=

Loss of continuity between values, memories, purpose, and actions.

---

# Mission

Detect:

- Identity Fragmentation
- Purpose Collapse
- Value Drift
- Cognitive Drift
- Narrative Breakdown

Before irreversible continuity loss occurs.

---

# Drift Categories

## D1

Value Drift

Question:

Do current actions still align with core values?

---

Indicators

- Principle Violations
- Conflicting Priorities
- Ethical Inconsistency

---

## D2

Purpose Drift

Question:

Do current activities still support long-term direction?

---

Indicators

- Mission Divergence
- Goal Instability
- Direction Loss

---

## D3

Memory Drift

Question:

Can the story still be reconstructed?

---

Indicators

- Context Loss
- Historical Disconnection
- Knowledge Fragmentation

---

## D4

Cognitive Drift

Question:

Is thinking still coherent?

---

Indicators

- Contradictory Reasoning
- Reduced Reflection
- Meaning Degradation

---

## D5

Behavior Drift

Question:

Do actions reflect stated beliefs?

---

Indicators

- Action-Value Gap
- Chronic Inconsistency
- Repeated Self-Contradiction

---
